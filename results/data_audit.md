## Cleaning

- Whitespace is collapsed in every text (arXiv/PeerRead human texts keep hard line breaks and leading spaces; machine texts don't, which would be a formatting shortcut).
- BLOOMZ files: human text from `text`/`abstract`, machine text from `machine_answer`/`machine_abstract` (their `machine_text` field is the prompt + output).
- PeerRead: one row per review (`_h<k>`/`_m<k>`), group = venue + paper id (ids normalised: '104' = 104.0); duplicate reviews are kept once; at most max_per_cell // 4 papers per cell (~4 machine reviews each).
